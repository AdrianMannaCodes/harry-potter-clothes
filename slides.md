---

# Phonics Spell: Open vs Closed Syllables & Magic E

- **Closed syllable**: vowel followed by a consonant, short vowel sound (**hat**, **sock**, **jum-per**)
- **Open syllable**: syllable ends in a vowel, long sound (**ro-be**, **ti-ger**)
- **Magic E**: silent **e** makes the vowel say its name (**robe**, **cape**, **gloves**)

---

# Grammar: Singular vs Plural

- A robe **is** magical. 
- Two robes **are** magical.
- The sock **is** black. 
- The socks **are** black.

Rule: use **is** for one item, **are** for more than one.

---

# Speaking Challenge

- What is Harry wearing?
- What are you wearing?

Answer in full sentences: *Harry is wearing a robe. I am wearing a jumper.*

# Interactive Game: Help Dobby Sort the Clothes

- Group 3: Accessories (scarf, gloves, boots)

Students come up and sort the cards.
